    Help

    How does it work?

    Add files, type the limit you need, and press Make it fit. The app tries quality levels until each file is just under the limit, then shows you the result before you save it.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Everything happens on this PC. The app also works with no internet connection.

    Why couldn't it reach my limit?

    Some files can't get that small and stay readable. You can allow lower quality (in the side panel), choose a larger limit, or remove pages you don't need before shrinking a PDF.

    Text in my PDF became a picture

    When shrinking the pictures inside a PDF isn't enough, the app can turn whole pages into images, like a scan. The result tells you when this happened. Turn it off under PDF options if you need selectable text.

    Signed PDFs

    Shrinking a digitally signed PDF makes the signature invalid, so the app asks before it changes one. If the signature matters, ask the sender for a smaller signed copy.

    Protected PDFs

    If a protected PDF allows printing, the app makes a print-style copy with pages as images. If the owner blocked printing and changes, it can't be shrunk.

    iPhone HEIC photos

    Not supported yet. On the iPhone, open Settings › Photos and set “Transfer to Mac or PC” to Automatic, then copy the photo again to get a JPG. For future photos: Settings › Camera › Formats › Most Compatible.

    Word or PowerPoint files

    Save them as a PDF first (File › Save As › PDF), then add the PDF here.

    KB and MB

    Make It Fit counts 1 KB as 1,000 bytes and 1 MB as 1,000,000 bytes. A file that fits here also passes websites that count 1,024.
