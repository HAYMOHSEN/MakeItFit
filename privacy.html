Privacy policy

Make It Fit shrinks photos and PDFs on your own PC. Last updated: 4 October 2026.

Your files never leave your PC

Every file you add is processed inside the app on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, sent to a server, or shared with anyone. The app works without an internet connection.

No accounts, no tracking

Make It Fit has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not collect personal information of any kind.

What is stored on your PC

Only your app settings (such as your preferred size limit and theme) are saved, in the app's own local storage. Files you shrink are saved only where you choose to save them. Passwords you type for protected PDFs are used once and never stored.

Hidden information in photos and PDFs

When the app rewrites a file, it removes hidden data such as camera details and location coordinates in photos, and author or software details in PDFs, so you don't share more than you intend.
